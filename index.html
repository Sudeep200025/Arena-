Arena Animation GTB Nagar offers a wide range of creative programmes including Animation, VFX (Visual Effects), Video Editing, Game Design, Web Design, Graphic Design, UI/UX Design, and Digital Marketing — designed to transform your creative passion into a professional career.

Course durations vary depending on the programme. Short Term programmes may range from 1 month to 1 year, while advanced career courses like Animation & VFX can be longer. For exact timings and batch schedules, please contact the GTB Nagar centre directly.

Arena Animation GTB Nagar offers the following programme categories:

  • 2D and 3D Animation
  • VFX (Visual Effects)
  • Gaming / AR-VR
  • Video Editing & Motion Graphics
  • Web & Graphics
  • UI-UX Design
  • Digital Design & Marketing
  • Multimedia
  • Short Term Courses
  • Fast Track Courses
